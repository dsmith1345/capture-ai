4 · Estimating logic and boundaries

Current formulas

Aircraft acquisition: aircraft quantity × aircraft unit price.

Support package: initial package + max(0, aircraft quantity − included aircraft) × additional support price.

Fleet flight hours: aircraft quantity × annual hours per aircraft × modeled years.

Baseline fleet hours: aircraft quantity × 500 × modeled years.

Optional additional utilization charge: max(0, actual fleet hours − baseline fleet hours) × optional incremental price per flight hour.

Effective support revenue per flight hour: support package sales plus optional utilization charge, divided by modeled fleet flight hours.

What is not yet priced

Actual variable costs for maintenance, repairables, consumables, spares replenishment, manpower, engineering, training, support equipment, IT, transportation, and recurring facilities. These require authorized platform-specific cost, reliability, utilization, and contractual data.

Commercial and governance cautions

Do not treat the $6,667 initial-package or $3,000 additional-aircraft ratios as validated CPFH rates. Avoid double counting labor across Maintenance Planning & Management and Manpower & Personnel. Record customer facts separately from analyst assumptions. Require cost-estimator, product-support, engineering, and pricing-authority review before business use.
